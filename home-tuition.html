Personalised Learning at Home

H&Y Academy's home tuition option is designed for students who prefer dedicated academic support in their home environment.

The learning approach can be adjusted according to the student's standard, subjects, academic requirements and learning goals.

Home Tuition Features

Personal Attention

Dedicated teaching focused on the individual student's learning requirements.

Flexible Learning

Learning can be planned around the student's academic needs and suitable schedule.

Doubt Solving

Students can focus on difficult concepts and clarify their academic doubts.

Progress Guidance

Academic progress can be monitored to identify areas requiring additional attention.

Who Can Choose Home Tuition?

School Students

Students from Standard 1 to Standard 10 who require personalised academic support.

Science Students

Standard 11 and 12 Science students who need focused support in their subjects.

Subject Support

Students who need additional help with specific subjects or difficult topics.

Exam Preparation

Students looking for additional academic guidance during important examination preparation.
